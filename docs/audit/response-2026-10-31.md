## Governance layout

```
Safe (2-of-3)
 ├─ TimelockController (48h min delay; Safe proposes and executes; no admin)
 │    └─ owns FoboBurner        emergencyWithdraw, setRoute, setKeeper, setHook: 48h public delay
 └─ owns FoboFactoryGate          which owns FoboFactory
      ├─ allow(quote): only after probe() + 7,200 blocks (~24h) + unchanged sample
      ├─ disallow(quote): immediate
      └─ the factory's other owner functions pass through at once (pause, signer rotation)
```

Safe transactions, in order: 1 `burner.transferOwnership(timelock)` `<TX>` · 2–3 timelock schedule+execute `burner.acceptOwnership()` (delay 0) `<TX>` `<TX>` · 4–5 schedule+execute `updateDelay(172800)` `<TX>` `<TX>` · 6 `factory.transferOwnership(gate)` `<TX>` · 7 `gate.acceptFactory()` `<TX>` · 8 `limitOrders.setPayToken(ETH, boundedFeed, 90000, false, true)` `<TX>` · 9 `setStartSigner(0xB5E9fCB2016cEC6972cA3e11f81297D28747f8e0)` via the gate `<TX>` · 10 `revenue.setRegistrar(0x8d617e5D524A327449a6B0479742B5e830689dB7)` `<TX>`.

Rehearsed end to end on a mainnet fork first (`script/govern-rehearsal.sh`): all 8 governance transactions, then every allowed quote through probe → 7,200 blocks → allow (USDG, MSFT, TSLA, SPCX, GOOGL, AMZN, AAPL, NVDA, META: all ok), and the end state checked (owners, `getMinDelay() == 172800`, `ethUsd()` unchanged through the bounded feed, `emergencyWithdraw` and `setQuoteAllowed` straight from the Safe revert, pause through the gate immediate).

## Per finding

**M1 · Quote admitted with no on-chain check.** `FoboFactoryGate` is the factory's owner, so `setQuoteAllowed(quote, true)` is only reachable through it: `probe(quote, amount)` pulls a known amount and requires the balance to rise by exactly that amount (fee-on-transfer fails) with any call back into the gate reverting (transfer hooks fail); `allow(quote)` only after 7,200 blocks and only if the sample balance is unchanged without a transfer (rebasing fails); `disallow` is immediate; ETH and $FOBO need no probe. Stated limits: a callback to the payer isn't visible to the gate; a rebase netting to zero across the window would pass; the owner review stays. Allowed quotes are published in the README.
Verify: `src/FoboFactoryGate.sol`, `test/FactoryGate.t.sol`; on-chain `FoboFactory.owner() == <GATE_ADDRESS>`, `FoboFactoryGate.owner() == Safe`.

**M2 · Owner can withdraw the buyback funds.** FoboBurner's owner is the `TimelockController` (48h minimum delay; the Safe, now 2-of-3, is sole proposer and executor; no admin), so `emergencyWithdraw` and every other owner action on the burner is public 48h before it can execute. Docs say funds waiting for a buyback are owner-recoverable behind that delay.
Verify: `FoboBurner.owner() == <TIMELOCK_ADDRESS>`, `TimelockController.getMinDelay() == 172800`; https://fobo.fun/how-it-works.

**L1 · Creator's launch buy pays the base tax.** By design (the buy is inside the launch transaction; the launch tax stops others front-running the creator); the rule is in the immutable hook. Disclosed.
Verify: https://fobo.fun/how-it-works, FAQ "Can the creator buy in?" ("…pays the normal 2.5% tax, not the launch tax…"); the launch form's note under "First buy".

**L2 · One hot signing key.** The signer is now an AWS KMS key (secp256k1, origin `AWS_KMS`, non-exportable) in FOBO's own account; the server holds a sign-only identity; every signature is a CloudTrail event; rotation is a new key and one `setStartSigner`. The site records every start it signs and alerts the admins on any launch without one.
Verify: `docs/audit/kms-evidence-2026-10-31.json` (DescribeKey, public key, derived address, IAM policy); on-chain `FoboFactory.startSigner() == 0xB5E9fCB2016cEC6972cA3e11f81297D28747f8e0`.

**L3 · Failed raise swallowed.** Every new `RaiseFailed` alerts the admins with the token and revert reason; a second on the same pool within 24h is an incident. None has happened since launch.
Verify: `apps/web/src/lib/adminAlerts.ts` and its tests (private repo; excerpt on request).

**L4 · ~1.2M gas on a raise block.** Deliberate; documented for integrators with "estimate on a raise block".
Verify: README "Integrating".

**L5 · Referral credited to `tx.origin`.** Acknowledged: the lookup is in the hook, which has no upgrade path (I1). Affects smart-wallet and bundled swaps only; the share goes to the team, never a third party. Disclosed on the referral page and in the README.
Verify: https://fobo.fun/refer; README "Integrating".

**L6 · Floor surplus leaves the token.** Disclosed: surplus beyond what the circulating supply could sell into leaves the pool; for a launched token it buys and burns $FOBO; in $FOBO's own pool it is team revenue.
Verify: https://fobo.fun/how-it-works.

**L7 · Registrar key can divert referral fees.** The registrar is now an AWS KMS key (same evidence file); a single `registerBy` transaction binding more than 20 traders to one referrer alerts the admins. Recovery as the report describes.
Verify: `docs/audit/kms-evidence-2026-10-31.json`; on-chain `FoboRevenue.registrar() == 0x8d617e5D524A327449a6B0479742B5e830689dB7`.

**L8 · Feed not checked for a wrong answer.** `FoboBoundedFeed` (ownerless, all-immutable) forwards the live Chainlink ETH/USD feed (`0x78F3556b67E17Df817D51Ef5a990cDaF09E8d3A9`) unchanged but reverts outside $100–$100,000 or on a move over 50% against the previous round; set as ETH's feed with `setPayToken`. Open orders keep the pricing they were placed with (I8); new orders and the burner's peg check read through it at once. Note: `sequencerUptimeFeed` is unset on mainnet; Robinhood Chain has no Chainlink sequencer feed.
Verify: `src/FoboBoundedFeed.sol`, `test/BoundedFeed.t.sol`; on-chain `FoboLimitOrders.payTokens(address(0)).feed == <FEED_ADDRESS>`, `FoboBoundedFeed.source() == 0x78F3…d3A9`.

**L9 · Fill at the limit, keeper keeps the rest.** True of the contract; fills are keeper-only, the only keeper charges gas + 20% capped by the order's max fee and passes any improvement to the owner. The order form says so.
Verify: order form, "Fills at your limit price or better. The keeper charges its gas cost plus 20%, up to your max fee."

**I3 · Scope and method.** The test suite is public: `forge test` → 35 suites, 397 passed (fuzz at 1000 runs), including `testFuzz_everyoneCanAlwaysExitAboveTheFloor`, `testFuzz_floorHoldsAcrossTranches` and `AuditInv.testFuzz_accountingAndFloors`.

**I5 · Fee cap is per fill.** Labelled "Max fee per fill" with "An order can fill in parts; each fill's fee is at most this."

**I6 · Exit guarantee margin.** `testFuzz_wholeCirculatingSupplySellsInOneSwap` and `testFuzz_wholeCirculatingSupplySellsInManySmallSwaps` (`test/Fobo.t.sol`), 1000 runs each: no sell ever reverts, the seller ends with zero tokens, the price ends within one tick of the floor tick (v4 rounding on the last step), 2–5 ticks after raises (the rounding margin the report describes).
Verify: `forge test --mt wholeCirculatingSupply`.

**I1, I2, I4, I7, I8.** Acknowledged with thanks; no change needed.
